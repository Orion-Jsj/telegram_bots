How it flows

1 Log in (code + optional 2FA) — session saved to tg.session
2 Pick groups / channels / chats and probe your rights
3 Choose content types to delete (default: everything protected)
4 Dry-run to see exactly what would go
5 Run & delete with a single confirmation

What it can delete

Private chats: always — your side and the other side (revoke).
Groups where you're owner/admin with delete rights: everyone's messages.
Everywhere else: only your own messages.

The Create tab tells you, per target, which right you're missing if a cleanup isn't possible.
